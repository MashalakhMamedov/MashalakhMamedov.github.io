Power electronics

3 kVA Single-Phase H-Bridge Inverter

Unipolar SPWM design and THD validation in MATLAB/Simulink

Designed for a 400 V DC input and a 230 V RMS, 50 Hz output target; the simulation produced ≈ 229.4 V RMS at unity PF and 213.5 V RMS at 0.5 lagging PF. 1.64 % output THD against a 3 % requirement — then an LC resonance under inductive load, diagnosed and damped, with the cost of the fix reported.
